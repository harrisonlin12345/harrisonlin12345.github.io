DIY MIDI DJ Controller

Custom Rekordbox MIDI controller built around an Arduino Micro. The design uses two MCP23017 I2C GPIO expanders for digital controls and two 74HC4067 multiplexers for analog inputs. Firmware maps buttons, encoders, faders, and potentiometers to MIDI messages.
